# Anti-brainrot project #1: HTML Frogger

Remember the days when you would just sit at your computer, programming, without even being connected to the internet? Just flowing, creating, solving problems in beautifully inoptimal ways, just to get it to work, and then improving them step by step. For me, that was back in 8th grade probably. But lately I've started to feel especially brainrotted. I was fantasizing of just whipping out my laptop mid flight and coding something functional up.

But then I realized I have free will (?), and that's exactly what I did.

The result was this HTML Frogger close with procedurally-generated levels. I love procedural generation, it was one of the first topics in CS that ever interested me.

This is a first in a series of hopefully many anti-brainrot projects, written (and successfully played) on an airplane in its entirety. Think of this as an art piece, a **"man and a machine against the bigger machine"** type of journey. Just me and my laptop, lost in space and time somewhere above Europe.
